## Review Checklist

- The YouTube video is public or intentionally unlisted.
- The thumbnail and video links work while signed out.
- The title, duration, audience, outcome, and prerequisites are complete.
- YouTube chapters match the chapter list.
- Captions and the repository transcript are available.
- Companion documentation links resolve.
- Screens and narration contain no sensitive or client-specific information.
